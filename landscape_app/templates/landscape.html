About Wall Writings

Wall writings are traces of human presence in public space: fragments of thought, resistance, humor, and identity left on walls, streets, and surfaces.

They take many forms such as slogans, poems, political messages, or spontaneous expressions, shaped by place and moment.

This project documents these inscriptions to build a living archive of urban expression.

Submission Guidelines
  • Upload clear images of wall writings
  • Enable location services for automatic location filling
  • Include accurate location details
  • Use short labels, such as poem, slogan, political
  • Add context or meaning if relevant
  • Uploading may take a little while
