Handwriting Task Analyzer

The early identification of cognitive changes consistent with Alzheimer's disease is essential for timely management and effective care. This online application examines handwriting analysis through a writing test, aiming to become a future accessible and non-invasive screening method for early identification of potential issues in a non-clinical setting. The test involves drawing on a white paper, taking a picture of the drawing, and then uploading it. The drawing could be either of the following two tests:

1. The Clock Drawing Test (09:05)

Draw the circle of a clock, the numbers, and then place the two hands to indicate the time 09:05.

2. The Continuous Line Test

Draw two points, connected with a horizontal line, pressing this horizontal line continuously with your pen four times.

To complete the process, save the drawing in a .jpg, .jpeg, or .png file. Then, upload the photo to the website and click the "Calculate Alzheimer's Risk" button.

DISCLAIMER

We inform you that this is a pilot application, only analyzing the photos you upload without storing any information about the user. The calculated probabilities for Alzheimer's disease are intended to assist the healthcare professional and do not constitute a clinically accredited diagnosis.

For more information, please read our scientific article:

Boumpi, M. Dalakleidi, K. V., Pavlopoulos, J. (2025) From Pen to Prediction: Handwriting-Based Alzheimer’s Detection. In IEEE 25th International Conference on Bioinformatics and Bioengineering (BIBE), pages 671-675, Athens, Greece, 2025, https://doi.org/10.1109/BIBE66822.2025.00116
